Summary in English · Calabria, Brazil and Ohio

The Suraci family of Laganadi, and its Sorage branch in Brazil.

Luigi Suraci was born in Laganadi, in the province of Reggio Calabria, in December 1881. Everything points to him being the Luiz Sorage who married in Brazil in 1902 and died there in 1936. His uncle Angelo went to Cleveland, Ohio. This is a summary in English; the full page, with every source, is in Portuguese.

01 / Laganadi, Reggio Calabria

The family

Laganadi is a small village on the slopes of the Aspromonte, about ten kilometres north-east of Reggio Calabria. Its civil records are held by the Archivio di Stato di Reggio Calabria, and its parish books by the diocesan archive of Reggio Calabria-Bova.

Domenico Surace (died 1863) and Grazia Romeo (died 1875) had at least six children there: Alessio (1841), Caterina (died 1899), Angelo (1847), Antonio (1849), Anna and Giuseppe. documented

Antonio Suraci, a carpenter, had lost his first wife and a newborn daughter in August 1877. In 1878 he married Francesca Versaci, a spinner. They had two known sons: Michele Domenico, born 4 August 1879, and Luigi, born 26 December 1881 according to the civil record. documented The parish book seems to give the 25th, and in Brazil the family remembered his birthday as Christmas Day.

02 / The American branch

Angelo Surace in Cleveland

Angelo Suraci, Antonio's brother and Luigi's uncle, was born in Laganadi on 8 March 1847 and married Anna Princi in 1877. Their children born in Laganadi include Domenico (1878), Francesco (1883) and Rosa (1887). On 4 June 1909, aged 62, Angelo arrived at Ellis Island on the SS Neckar from Naples, with his wife and four children, bound for Cleveland, Ohio, where his son Francesco already lived. He died in Cleveland on 31 March 1914; his death certificate spells his name Angelo Serace. very likely

The link between the Angelo of Laganadi and the Angelo of Cleveland rests on three records that fit together: the 1909 passenger list (Laganadi, wife Princi, children with matching names and ages), the Ohio death certificate (parents Domenico and Grazia) and his 1847 birth record. His 1877 civil marriage record has not been seen yet.

If you descend from Angelo Surace and Anna Princi of Cleveland, you are very likely a cousin of the Brazilian Sorage family.

03 / Carangola, Minas Gerais

Luigi in Brazil

On 12 April 1902, on a farm in the district of Carangola, “Luiz Sorate”, aged 20, a farm worker, married Isolina Vecchia, born in 1884 in Casalbuttano ed Uniti, near Cremona. He declared himself born in the “City of Mecina”, which sent the family looking in Messina, Sicily, for almost twenty years. He died in Carangola on 26 September 1936 as Luiz Sorage, a merchant, leaving eight children. documented

That Luigi Suraci of Laganadi and Luiz Sorage of Carangola are the same man rests on the names of both parents, his exact ages in 1902 and 1936, and the Christmas birthday. No single record says both things yet. very likely

04 / The open question

What we don't know

After December 1881 Antonio, Francesca and their sons vanish from the Laganadi records, while Antonio's brothers and sisters keep appearing in them. The simplest explanation is that the four left together, probably in the 1880s. hypothesis

We don't know when they left or through which port, where Antonio and Francesca died (before 1902), or what became of Michele. open
